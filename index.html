About & Rules

The complete scoring system — transparent and reproducible

🛡 What is LANDGUARD?

LANDGUARD is an educational hackathon prototype that simulates a land conflict intelligence system. It helps users understand what kinds of inconsistencies can appear in land records, and how they might be identified through transparent, rule-based checks.

The system operates entirely on synthetic, fictional demo data. It does not connect to any government database, does not access real land records, and does not make legal determinations. No real person's information is used or referenced.

📋 All Rules Reference

Transparent & Reproducible — No black-box AI

Risk scores are calculated by summing the points of all triggered rules. Maximum score is 100. Scores are capped if multiple rules would exceed this.

Rule ID Rule Name Severity Points Description
RULE-01 Ownership Mismatch HIGH +40 Deed and mutation records show different owner names for the same plot.
RULE-02 Duplicate Active Deeds HIGH +45 Multiple deeds with Active status exist for the same plot.
RULE-03 Missing Mutation Record MEDIUM +15 No mutation record found after deed registration.
RULE-04 Unlinked Transfer Record MEDIUM +10 Transfer history entry lacks corresponding deed or mutation cross-reference.
RULE-05 Unverified Deed MEDIUM +10 Deed verification status is other than "Verified".
RULE-06 Chronological Inconsistency HIGH +20 Transfer date predates its referenced deed registration date.
RULE-07 Missing Critical Fields LOW +5/field (max 20) Required fields (area, consideration, witness) are absent from the record.
Risk Level Thresholds:
Score 0 = No Conflict Detected  |  1–19 = Low Risk  |  20–49 = Medium Risk  |  50–100 = High Risk  |  RULE-07 only (Incomplete data) = Incomplete Data

⚠ What LANDGUARD Cannot Do

  • It cannot access, query, or verify real government land records.
  • It cannot make legally binding ownership determinations.
  • It cannot confirm or deny that fraud has occurred.
  • It cannot replace advice from a qualified legal professional or licensed surveyor.
  • Results are based only on the fictional demo dataset included with this prototype.

🔬 Technology Stack

  • Frontend: HTML5, CSS3 (Custom Design Tokens, Light/Dark Modes), Vanilla JavaScript ES6+
  • Language & Localization: Full bilingual (Bangla & English) support with high-quality typography
  • Data: Local synthetic JavaScript records representing land conflict patterns
  • Backend: None required — 100% transparent client-side evaluation engine
  • Dependencies: Zero external frameworks or paid APIs — runs locally in any modern browser

🎓 Hackathon Context

LANDGUARD was built as a prototype for the Land Technology Innovation Hackathon. It demonstrates how transparent, rule-based logic can surface potential inconsistencies in land record datasets, and how a responsible, explainable approach can be taken without making unsubstantiated legal claims. The scoring algorithm is fully documented and reproducible from the rules above.
